# **App Name**: Ethiopia EOC Dashboard

## Core Features:

- Weather Alerts: Display real-time weather alerts for major Ethiopian cities using public weather APIs.
- Humanitarian News Feed: Curate and display the latest humanitarian news specific to Ethiopia from ReliefWeb and similar services.
- Health Alerts: Integrate global public health alerts relevant to Ethiopia.
- Interactive Incident Map: Display an interactive incident map of Ethiopia, populated with real-time data synced across clients.
- AI-Powered Summary: Employ an LLM as a tool to summarize key details from multiple data sources related to specific incidents or alerts for better situational awareness.  Summaries would include extracted locations and casualty figures.
- Ethiopia News Feed: Curate general news about Ethiopia.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Vibrant orange (#E67E22) to convey a sense of urgency and energy, without being alarming; inspired by the colors of the Ethiopian flag but with higher brightness.
- Background color: Light gray (#EEEEEE), a desaturated tone of orange, providing a clean and neutral backdrop to ensure readability and focus.
- Accent color: Muted red (#C0392B), for critical alerts and key interactive elements.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter', a grotesque-style sans-serif font known for its modern and neutral look, ensuring legibility and clarity across the dashboard.
- Use clear, minimalist icons to represent different types of alerts, incidents, and data points. Icons should follow a consistent style and color scheme to enhance visual communication and overall usability.
- Implement a responsive, card-based layout that organizes information into distinct modules for weather alerts, news feeds, health alerts, and the interactive incident map. Use clear headings and consistent spacing to maintain a balanced and accessible user experience.
- Incorporate subtle transitions and animations to enhance the user experience without overwhelming the operator. Examples include fading in new alerts, highlighting key data points, and smoothly zooming on the interactive map.
